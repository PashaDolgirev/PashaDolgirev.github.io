Pavel Dolgirev

Theoretical physicist

I am a theoretical physicist aiming at breakthrough science and technology.

My main interests today are reinforcement learning for quantum and photonic hardware, to raise the algorithmic utility of these devices, and agentic AI that automates scientific discovery, the discovery of physical laws in particular. Much of my work centers on real materials and their design, often together with experimental groups.

  • Postdoctoral Fellow, Theoretical Quantum Optics, University of Maryland
  • Ph.D. in Physics, Harvard University, with Mikhail Lukin and Eugene Demler
  • Previously Visiting Scientist at QuEra Computing, leading AI and Hamiltonian-learning work
